## Playback

- Added previous and next video controls, 10-second rewind and forward, and play or pause to the video overlay and fullscreen.
- Media notifications and compatible lock screens use the service’s Media3 queue for previous and next actions.
- When YouTube returns a successful response without a playable stream, the player tries its existing fallback.

## Watch History

- Open Watch History from Library. Entries stay on the device and appear after at least three seconds of playback.
- Choose 7, 14, or 30 days of retention. You can resume videos, remove individual entries, or clear the list after confirmation.

## App icon

- The blue play symbol and cyan orbit are used in the Android launcher, the in-app logo, the TV banner, and the repository README.
